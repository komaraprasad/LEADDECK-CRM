First-time setup

1
Download the extension

Open the tool and click its Download extension button, then unzip it.

2
Load it in your browser

Go to edge://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked.

3
Sign in & refresh

Log in to LinkedIn (Sales Navigator for TalentPulse) in that browser, then refresh the tool.

Tip: if a tool says “extension not detected” inside LeadDeck, use Open in new tab — extensions are most reliable in a full tab.
